How to tell them apart

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first, and the report came through the app, by email, or through booking-v2. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month swap: 7 April becomes 4 July.
  • hint: the tenant writes the day first and runs booking-v3, and the report came through the form. The form tells people to type month first, then reads what they typed day first. Day and month swap here too, and the parser fix does not touch the form.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day and the month stays. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” A parser that swaps day and month cannot do that. Two of these reports name different time zones for the same meeting, so look at time-zone handling first.

Closing #4471 did not close the one-day problem. Keep its ticket open until someone fixes it.
